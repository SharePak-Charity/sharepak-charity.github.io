Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

The SharePak management system is an internal application operated by SharePak Charity for its own staff. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data we access

The application connects to a single Google Drive account owned by SharePak Charity. It uses Google Drive only to store, list and remove the charity's own files: database backups, application logs and generated reports. It does not access any other user's Google account or data.

How we use it

Files are written to and read from the charity's Drive solely to keep off-site backups and records of the charity's own operations. We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

Storage and security

The Drive authorization token is stored on the charity's own server and is never shared. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings.
